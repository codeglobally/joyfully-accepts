# Code of conduct

Joyfully Accepts is a project about bringing people together, so we'd like the space around it to feel the same: welcoming, patient and kind.

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome to take part, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, sex characteristics, sexual orientation, nationality, religion, appearance, education, experience or background. We'll do our best to make taking part a friendly, harassment-free experience for all.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to code or to GitHub.
- Respect different opinions and experiences, and disagree politely.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Focus on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to anyone affected, and learn from them.
- Look after people's privacy. Never post someone else's personal details, and that includes the names, emails and invitation codes of anyone's wedding guests.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, put-downs, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their clear permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

Everywhere in this project: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour worries you, open the **⋯** menu on their comment, issue or pull request and choose **Report content**. You can send the report to the project's maintainer, to GitHub, or to both. Reports to the maintainer are kept private.

Reports will be read promptly and handled fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened, the maintainer may:

1. **Have a word.** A private note explaining what was wrong, sometimes with a request for a public apology.
2. **Give a warning.** A clear warning, with consequences if the behaviour continues.
3. **Ban for a while.** A temporary break from interacting with the project.
4. **Ban for good.** For serious or repeated harm, including any harassment.

Comments, commits and issues that break this code may be edited, hidden or removed.
